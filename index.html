CertifyStudio Pro - Quick Start Guide

1
Select or Upload Background Template Choose one of the 3 built-in vector background presets (*Golden Elegance*, *Modern Minimalist*, *Academic Crest*) or upload your custom background image (PNG/JPG).
2
Input Recipient List Type or paste recipient names into the text area (one name per line). Click "Load Demo Names" to test immediately. Use the pagination arrows (`< Prev / Next >`) to preview individual certificates.
3
Drag & Drop & Custom Typography Click and drag recipient names, date, badge ID, or signature directly on the live canvas. Automatic blue dashed snap lines help align elements to 50% horizontal and vertical axes. Customize font styles, sizes, uppercase/title case, and hex colors in the Styling tab.
4
Single PNG or Bulk ZIP Export Click "Download Single" for a high-res image of the current preview, or click "Generate & Download ZIP" to automatically process and package all certificates into a ZIP file.
